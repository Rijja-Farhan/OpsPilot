# Dispute and Refund Policy (v1)

## Filing window
A customer must file a card or payment dispute within **60 days** of the transaction date. Disputes filed after 60 days are rejected unless fraud is confirmed.

## Process
1. Support records the dispute and the disputed amount.
2. Provisional credit is **not** given while the dispute is open.
3. Northwind Pay contacts the merchant; the merchant has 30 days to respond.

## Refunds
Merchant refunds appear within 10 business days. After that, the customer may open a refund dispute.
