# Dispute and Refund Policy (v2)

## Filing window
A customer must file a card or payment dispute within **120 days** of the transaction date. Disputes about unauthorised transactions follow the Fraud Policy instead and have no filing window.

## Process
1. Support records the dispute and the disputed amount.
2. Provisional credit of up to 500 GBP is given within 5 business days of filing for eligible card disputes. Any provisional credit or refund decision requires human approval.
3. Northwind Pay contacts the merchant; the merchant has 20 days to respond.

## Refunds
Merchant refunds appear within 7 business days. After that, the customer may open a refund dispute.
