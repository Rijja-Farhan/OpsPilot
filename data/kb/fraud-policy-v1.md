# Fraud Policy (v1)

## Liability
A customer is not liable for unauthorised transactions reported within 13 months, unless they acted fraudulently or with gross negligence.

## Freeze, then investigate
When a customer reports a compromised card or account takeover, the card or account is frozen first and investigated afterwards. Freezes are applied by a human agent, never automatically by OpsPilot.

## Investigation
Fraud cases get priority P1 or P2. The fraud team decides within 10 business days and the customer is informed of the outcome in writing.
