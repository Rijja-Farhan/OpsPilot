# Fees Schedule (v2)

| Item | Fee |
|---|---|
| Domestic transfer | Free |
| International transfer | 2.50 GBP |
| ATM withdrawal abroad | 1% of amount, minimum 1.00 GBP, free up to 200 GBP per month |
| Replacement physical card | Free for the first replacement per year, then 5.00 GBP |
| Foreign exchange (card payment) | 0.5% above the mid-market rate |

Any fee waiver or refund of a fee requires human approval.
